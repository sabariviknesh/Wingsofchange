About us

Guidance, resources and belief — regardless of background.

Wings of Change is a volunteer-driven non-profit dedicated to mentoring and supporting underprivileged and marginalized students across Tamil Nadu. We work from a simple belief: talent is universal, but access to guidance, professional networks and encouragement is not. Our mentors close that gap.

To build a generation of empowered youth — guided by values, mentored by leaders, and driven by purpose. Our vision

02 Personalized mentoring

Founded by CEG, Anna University alumni Built by mentors, for mentees

Wings of Change began with the same question, asked again and again: bright students, full of ability, with no one to turn to and ask "what next?" Alumni who kept meeting that question decided to become the answer.

In partnership with Thisai

Where mentorship pointed to a gap, we built a tool to close it.

Years of one-on-one mentoring surfaced the same pattern: most school and college students don't know what career path to pursue — they just need someone, or something, to help them choose one. That insight, born directly out of Wings of Change's mentoring work, became the starting point for Thisai.

Today, Wings of Change mentees get the best of both: a human mentor who knows them, and an AI-backed career assessment from Thisai that turns "I'm not sure" into a clear, evidence-based direction — before the first mentoring conversation even begins.
